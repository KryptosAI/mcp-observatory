MCP Observatory is free and open source.

The former paid Observatory offers are retired. All scanner features, reports, CI, and local history are MIT licensed and require no account.

Start with a local scan

npx -y @kryptosai/mcp-observatory@latest

Run mcp-observatory dashboard to review local evidence, or mcp-observatory clearframe to prepare a review task. Task preparation does not launch an agent or upload evidence.

Get ClearFrame CLI or Mac

ClearFrame has its own account and usage terms. Observatory works without it.
