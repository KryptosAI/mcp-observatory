Privacy notice

Commercial requests are separate from product telemetry.

Use this page for pilot requests and partner deal registrations. The full open-source telemetry policy remains available in the repository.

What forms collect

Business contact information, company/domain, role, high-level MCP context, decision timing, referral source, and campaign attribution that you explicitly submit.

How it is used

To respond to the commercial request, schedule a fit call, and operate the sales pipeline. Qualified requests receive a reply within one business day.

What not to submit

Do not send credentials, source code, private URLs, customer data, production commands, or other sensitive technical material in a public form.

Commercial form data is not added to CLI telemetry, public reports, or Safety Index evidence. Read the complete privacy and telemetry policy before submitting.
