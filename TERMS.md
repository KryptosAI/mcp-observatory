# MCP Observatory use and migration

Updated October 21, 2026.

MCP Observatory product features are free software under the [MIT license](./LICENSE). Scanning, auditing, reports, CI, local history, downloads, and task preparation require no account or subscription. The MIT license governs the software, including its warranty disclaimer.

Only test systems you are authorized to inspect. Findings are technical evidence, not certification or a guarantee of security. Review commands, generated CI, and evidence before executing or sharing them.

## ClearFrame

ClearFrame is a separate optional product. Preparing a local task does not enroll you, start inference, or create a payment obligation. Your separately initiated ClearFrame use follows its current account and usage terms.

## Existing hosted accounts

Legacy hosted identity, receipt access, and billing management remain during migration. Local source changes do not cancel subscriptions or move hosted records. Existing paid-period rights and previously agreed terms remain subject to the account's existing agreement until the hosted and billing transition is completed. Export records you need and use existing billing management or use the contact in the privacy notice for account-specific help. Do not assume an Observatory GitHub identity has merged with a ClearFrame identity.

Local evidence stays on your disk until you delete it. Legacy hosted retention continues as displayed in that service until its migration is deployed. Review [privacy details](./PRIVACY.md) before uploading.
