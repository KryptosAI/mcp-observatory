# MCP Observatory Terms

Effective September 1, 2026.

MCP Observatory is an open-core product. The local package is MIT-licensed and remains free and unlimited. Running it locally does not create a subscription, require telemetry, or create a retroactive payment obligation. These terms govern the MCP Observatory hosted service and Release Gate Pilot.

## Hosted Service

A signed-in user may upload one free hosted snapshot under the limits shown in the product. Individual Pro costs `$29` per month for one user and adds the hosted limits and features displayed at checkout, including retained history and hosted CI ingestion.

Paid subscriptions begin only after the user explicitly confirms checkout. They renew monthly at the then-disclosed price until canceled. Taxes may be added where required. Payment processing is handled by Stripe; the checkout or payment record may include additional merchant disclosures required by Stripe or applicable law.

The account owner may manage billing and cancel through the hosted billing portal. Cancellation stops the next renewal and takes effect at the end of the paid billing period unless law requires otherwise. Access may become read-only after a payment failure and returns to the free entitlement after the applicable grace or paid period ends.

Except where law requires otherwise, subscription charges are non-refundable once a billing period begins. MCP Observatory may issue a refund or credit for duplicate charges, verified billing errors, or material service failure at its discretion. Request billing help promptly through the contact listed in the [privacy notice](./PRIVACY.md).

## Hosted Data

The free service keeps the latest snapshot for up to seven days. Individual Pro keeps eligible scan history for up to 90 days while the subscription is active. The service may delete data after its retention deadline, when an account is downgraded, or when needed to enforce these terms. Users should download artifacts they need before canceling or downgrading.

Hosted artifacts are used to provide the requested dashboard, history, CI, and support features. MCP Observatory does not claim ownership of uploaded artifacts. Users must have authority to upload them and must not upload secrets, credentials, source files, personal customer data, raw MCP messages, or material they are not authorized to process. The [privacy notice](./PRIVACY.md) describes telemetry and hosted-service data handling separately.

## Acceptable Use

Users may not use the hosted service to violate law; probe systems without authorization; facilitate destructive exploitation; distribute malware; evade access controls or quotas; impersonate another person or organization; upload secrets or unlawfully obtained data; interfere with the service; or impose excessive automated load. MCP Observatory may throttle, suspend, or terminate abusive access while preserving any remedies required by law.

## Service Boundary

Hosted results and Safety Index evidence are engineering signals, not certification, endorsement, legal advice, compliance attestation, penetration-test results, or a guarantee that an MCP server is safe. Users remain responsible for deployment and release decisions. The hosted service is provided on an as-available basis to the extent permitted by law.

Telemetry choices do not affect eligibility for free or paid hosted functionality. Local OSS use remains available if the hosted service is unavailable or an account is suspended.

## MCP Release Gate Pilot

The public services offer is `$15,000` for 1-3 named MCP servers over ten business days. It produces private safe-mode evidence, an approve/gate/defer decision, CI/SARIF handoff, and owner-ready remediation. Broader work is separately scoped only after that release decision.

Pilot work begins only after a written agreement, accepted proposal, or purchase order identifies the contracting parties, scope, exclusions, payment schedule, and handling of private material. Unless that agreement says otherwise, payment is 50% at signing and 50% before the final readout. The pilot is not destructive testing, certification, or compliance attestation.
