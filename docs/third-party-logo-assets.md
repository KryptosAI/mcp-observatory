# Third-party logo assets

The generated technology-mark assets in `dashboard/proof-logos/` come from
[Simple Icons 16.29.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.29.0),
which is distributed under CC0-1.0. `scripts/build-dashboard.ts` writes the
selected SVGs from the pinned development dependency so dashboard builds remain
reproducible and same-origin.

The Microsoft, Google, Cloudflare, Accenture, Cisco, and Oracle wordmarks are
pre-existing project assets. Brand names and marks remain the property of their
respective owners.
