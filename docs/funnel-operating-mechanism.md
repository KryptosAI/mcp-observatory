# Weekly Funnel Operating Mechanism

Every Friday, record qualified buyer forms, booked meetings, held meetings, proposals, deposits, paid pilots, registered partner opportunities, partner calls, active partners, qualified buyer introductions, source attribution, and median first-response time.

Add buyer objections, missing proof, the next experiment, a named owner, and due date. Do not count raw downloads, stars, anonymous telemetry, or page views as commercial traction. Use CLI campaign attribution only for OSS distribution; never add raw telemetry, inferred identity, private URLs, hostnames, or credentials to HubSpot.

## Decision milestones

- Day 30: HubSpot intake and evidence sample live; sales kit complete; three qualified partner conversations.
- Day 90: ten qualified buyer conversations; three repeat external organizations using Observatory or CI; one active channel partner.
- Day 180: a paid pilot or signed contract and one channel producing repeatable qualified opportunities.

If the Day-180 milestone is missed, move Observatory to OSS-maintenance mode and stop treating it as the full-time venture bet.
