# MCP Release Gate Pilot Sales Kit

## Executive brief

MCP Observatory helps platform and security owners decide which MCP dependencies may enter production agent paths. The fixed Release Gate Pilot costs `$15,000`, covers 1-3 critical servers, and finishes in ten business days with evidence, CI policy, SARIF, remediation priorities, and an approve/gate/defer decision.

## Qualification checklist

Proceed only when a named platform, security, or engineering owner exists; MCP use is private, pre-production, or production; a decision is due within 60 days; and the buyer accepts the fixed scope.

## Discovery and demo script

1. Confirm the agent workflow and dependency approval decision.
2. Identify the accountable owner and deployment deadline.
3. Explain the safe-mode boundary and approve/gate/defer deliverable.
4. Show the illustrative evidence pack, not an invented customer claim.
5. Quote `$15,000`, ten business days, 50% at signing, and 50% before final readout.

## SOW and proposal requirements

Before accepting payment, use a written agreement naming the legal counterparty, target servers, safe-mode boundary, excluded activities, deliverables, timeline, payment milestones, customer responsibilities, confidentiality, and ownership of private artifacts.
